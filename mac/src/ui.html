Settings

Playback

When a stream stutters, Beeldbuis switches to the next lower quality by itself. You can always pick a quality by hand with the Quality button under the player.

Also under the player: Aa next to CC (there it also has Timing, to move the subtitles of the film you are watching). Each profile has its own style. On the iPhone in full screen, iOS draws subtitles in its own style (iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Subtitles & Captioning). The TV app has its own subtitle menu (Settings → Subtitle style, or ▲ while a film plays).

Live TV

While you watch live TV, the Mac keeps this much of the channel on disk (about 2–4 GB per hour in Full HD), so you can pause, go 15 s back with ← or 30 s on with →, and jump back to live with l. The TV app uses the same buffer when it is linked to this Mac. Further back: ↺ watch from start (catch-up).

Recording

Live sport (matches, races, fights) keeps recording a while longer, for overtime and late finishes. While a recording runs or one starts within 12 hours, Beeldbuis keeps this Mac from falling asleep (the screen may still go dark). Keep it on the charger with the lid open: a closed lid still sends a MacBook to sleep.

Channel categories

Untick what you never watch; those channels disappear from the lists and the guide.
